Initial Meeting

We start by getting to know your business, goals, and target audience. This step allows us to understand your vision and ensures the final product truly represents your brand.



Agreement and Planning

Once we’ve discussed your needs, we provide a detailed project proposal with timelines, deliverables, and pricing. After reaching an agreement, we map out the structure and design of your website to ensure a smooth workflow.
